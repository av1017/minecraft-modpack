Traveler's Backpack

A backpack you wear on your back. Beyond extra storage, it carries two fluid tanks, a sleeping bag, tool slots, and upgrade slots that add a crafting grid, furnace, magnet, auto-feeding, and more. Upgrade it through five tiers, from leather to netherite, or craft a themed backpack with its own special ability.

Your First Backpack

Three steps: a sleeping bag, two tanks, then the backpack. In total: 4 leather, 1 string, 3 wool, a chest, 12 glass, and 4 iron ingots.

There are also versions without tanks (a chest in the middle row instead of tanks and chest) and without a sleeping bag. The recipe book shows all of them. You can add tanks later with the Tanks upgrade.
  • Craft a sleeping bag
  • Craft 2 Backpack Tanks
  • Craft the Traveler's Backpack

Wearing It

Do thisHow
Put it onHold it in your main hand and right-click to open it, then click Equip Backpack. It now sits on your back, and your hands are free.
Open itG in the Cobblemon world (moved from B so it doesn't clash with the map). In Our World it should be B. Or click the backpack icon in your inventory.
Take it offOpen it, click the three dots button to show more buttons, then Unequip. You need a free inventory slot.
Place it as a blockHold it and sneak + right-click the ground. Right-click the placed backpack to open it, and break it to pick it back up with everything inside.
Inside the screenButtons sort (hold Shift to change the sort order), quick-stack into the backpack, and move everything between the backpack and your inventory. In settings you can mark slots to skip when sorting, or reserve slots for one item.
Hide it"Hide Backpack" in its screen stops it showing on your back.
One worn backpack at a time. Extras can be carried as items or placed as blocks.
  • Equip it and open it with its key
  • Place it on the ground and pick it back up

Tiers

Each tier adds a row of 9 storage slots, plus more upgrade and tool slots. Tank space grows with it: 1 bucket per storage row.

TierStorageEach tank holdsTier upgrade recipe
Leather27 slots (3 rows)3 bucketsWhere every backpack starts
Iron36 slots4 buckets8 iron ingots around a Blank Upgrade
Gold45 slots5 buckets8 gold ingots around a Blank Upgrade
Diamond54 slots6 buckets8 diamonds around a Blank Upgrade
Netherite63 slots7 bucketsSmithing table: netherite upgrade template + Blank Upgrade + netherite ingot

Applying a tier

  1. Craft a Blank Upgrade: 1 leather in the middle with 4 string around it (top, bottom, left, right).
  2. Turn it into the next tier's upgrade (table above). Tiers go in order: leather → iron → gold → diamond → netherite.
  3. At a smithing table, put leather in the template slot, your backpack in the middle, and the tier upgrade last. Your items stay inside.

Storage and tanks are read from the mod's config (27 slots at leather, one row per tier, 1 bucket per row). Exact upgrade and tool slot counts per tier show in-game.

  • Upgrade to Iron
  • Upgrade to Diamond

Upgrades

Functionality upgrades go in the slots on the right side of the backpack screen. Each one starts with a Blank Upgrade. Many only work while the backpack is worn. Toggle them on and off from the screen, or bind the "Toggle Upgrade" keys in Controls.

  • Craft a hose and fill a tank with water

Sleeping Bag

  1. Sleep anywhere: open the worn backpack, click the three dots, then Use Sleeping Bag. You can also place the backpack and unfold the bag beside it.
  2. It does not move your spawn point. It just skips the night. Your bed at home stays your respawn.
  3. Change its color: place the backpack, hold a different sleeping bag, and sneak + right-click the backpack. Shift-click the sleeping bag button to detach it.
On LAN, the night only skips when both players sleep, in a bed or a sleeping bag.
  • Skip a night with the sleeping bag

Themed Backpacks

Check the recipe book for each layout; for example, the Bee backpack is the standard pack surrounded by honeycomb and honey bottles. Turn the ability on in the backpack's screen.

If You Die

  1. The backpack is placed as a block where you died, and a chat message gives its coordinates. Write them down or make a map waypoint.
  2. Dying in the void spawns it floating above the bottom of the world instead of losing it.
  3. Still lost? The mod saves a copy of every backpack you die with. The host can restore it with /tb restore <player> <backpack file>, which needs cheats on (Open to LAN → Allow Cheats).

First Steps

What to do in your first hour.

  1. Pick your starter: press I. A starter screen opens with Pokémon from every region, Kanto through Paldea, plus a Random option. Choose one and click I choose you! Each player picks their own.
  2. Send it out: press R to throw your selected Pokémon out. Press it again while looking at it to call it back. ↑/↓ choose which party member R sends.
  3. Find apricorn trees. Their fruit, apricorns, is what Poké Balls are made from. They grow in forests, grasslands, hills, deserts, jungles, and snowy forests. Grab every color you see.
  4. Mine copper and iron. Basic Poké Balls need copper, Great Balls need iron, and the PC and Healing Machine need both.
  5. Mark your base with B, so you can always find it on the map. (B is the map's key here; the backpack opens with G in this world.)
  • Choose a starter (I)
  • Find an apricorn tree and collect apricorns
  • Set a waypoint at your base (B)

Catching

Poké Balls are four apricorns around a metal ingot. Better metal makes a better ball.

How to catch one

  1. Weaken it first. Battle the wild Pokémon (see Battling below) and bring its HP down. Low HP makes a catch much more likely.
  2. Throw a ball. Hold the Poké Ball and right-click to throw it at the Pokémon.
  3. Wait for the shakes. It either breaks free, and you try again, or gets caught and joins your party. A full party of 6 sends new catches to your PC.
Tip: save Ultra Balls (gold) for rare or strong Pokémon, and use plain Poké Balls (copper) on common ones.
  • Craft your first Poké Balls
  • Catch a wild Pokémon
  • Fill a party of 6

Battling

Battles work like the main Pokémon games: turns, moves, and switching.

  1. Start a battle: look at a wild Pokémon and press R to throw your selected Pokémon at it. A battle screen opens.
  2. Pick moves each turn. Type matchups matter (Water beats Fire, Fire beats Grass, and so on). You can also switch Pokémon or use items.
  3. Win for experience. Pokémon level up, learn moves, and evolve. Check your party anytime with I.
Cobblemon also supports battles between players, so the two of you can battle each other over LAN.
  • Win your first wild battle
  • Get a Pokémon to evolve

Healing & Storage

Two blocks every base needs: a PC for Pokémon beyond your party of 6, and a Healing Machine.

The Healing Machine's long ingredient chain

The Healing Machine needs a Max Revive, which takes a few steps. Revival Herb and Vivichoke are Cobblemon plants that grow in the wild. The cooking steps use Cobblemon's Campfire Pot; check the recipe book for it.

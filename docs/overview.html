Playing together

  1. Host: open the world, press Esc, click Open to LAN, then Start LAN World.
  2. The other player: open the same launcher profile, go to Multiplayer, and pick the world under LAN World(s).
  3. Spawned as a brand-new player? Leave right away; your real progress is still saved. Both of you fully quit Minecraft and the launcher, reopen, and rejoin.

When the mods change

Close Minecraft, then git pull in the modpack folder and double-click Sync Our World.bat or Sync Cobblemon.bat. Both PCs need the same mods for LAN to connect. Details are on GitHub.

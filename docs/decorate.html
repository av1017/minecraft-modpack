Finding Pieces

  1. Open your inventory. The REI item list is on the right.
  2. Type @ and a mod's name in REI's search bar at the bottom, like @handcrafted or @macaw, to see only that mod's blocks.
  3. Hover a piece and press Y for its recipe (or click it). U shows what it's used in.
  4. Press A on a piece to favorite it, so it stays pinned while you plan a room.

Good to Know

  1. Both players need the same mods to see these blocks over LAN. The sync handles that.
  2. Removing a decoration mod later deletes everything built with it, so the world gets backed up before any such change.
  3. These blocks keep their own textures. Faithful 32x only covers vanilla blocks, so mod pieces look a touch softer. They're all vanilla-styled, so they still fit.
  4. Our World has the first five mods. The other five don't exist for its newer Minecraft version yet.

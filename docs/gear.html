The Short Answer

Netheritevanilla · best all-round

Full protection (20), high toughness (3 per piece), knockback resistance, and it can't burn in lava. Put Protection IV, Unbreaking III, and Mending on it.

Heavy ArmorImmersive Armors · tank

18 armor with 4 toughness per piece, more than netherite's 3, and almost no knockback. The catch: you move slower.

Divine ArmorImmersive Armors · lifesaver

18 armor, and a full set completely blocks one attack every minute. Great for boss fights and creeper surprises. Dyeable.

Steampunk ArmorImmersive Armors · explorer

Only 14 armor, but a double jump, no fall damage, a hostile-mob radar while sneaking, and a clock and compass on screen.

Prismarine ArmorImmersive Armors · oceans

Diamond-level 20 armor, spikes that hurt attackers, and Depth Strider for fast swimming. A little slower on land.

Jousting set + StechhelmEpic Knights · knight

22 armor (more than you need; see below) with diamond-level toughness and very high knockback resistance. The best-looking tank.

Smithing Templates

Templates are like stamps. At a smithing table, a template + a piece of gear + a material makes the upgrade, and the template is used up. The Netherite Upgrade turns diamond gear into netherite; trim templates add a decorative pattern.

Three ways to get one

  1. Craft it from scratch (our Craftable Smithing Templates mod): 7 diamonds + a themed item + a block. See the table below.
  2. Copy one you have: the template + its block + 7 diamonds makes two (yours back plus a copy). Always copy your last one before you use it.
  3. Find one in a structure (the "Found in" column).

Trims

Trims are purely for looks. At a smithing table: trim template + armor piece (or tool) + a material for the color.

  • Vanilla materials: iron, copper, gold, emerald, redstone, lapis, amethyst, quartz, diamond, netherite.
  • Pokémon materials (Cobbled Armor Trims): Type Gems, Evolution Stones, and Tumblestones, for over 50,000 combinations. Go full Water-type with a Water Gem, or Fire with a Fire Stone.
  • Tools and weapons can be trimmed too.

Trims show best on vanilla armor and tools; some modded armor and weapons may not show them.

  • Trim armor with a Type Gem

Clothes & Hats

Trainer outfitsPoke Clothing

Dress as Ash (Kanto, Hoenn, Sinnoh…), Misty, Brock, Professor Oak, May, Jessie and James, or Team Rocket, Magma, and Aqua grunts. Make Cloth (1 wool + 4 string = 4 cloth, in any color), build a Tailoring Station (2 cloth over 4 planks), and sew outfits there. They're worn in your armor slots, so they replace armor.

Handmade clothesWeaver's Paradise

The full craft: grow or find cotton, spin it on a Spinning Jenny, sew at the Clothcrafting Station (a little minigame), then dye at the Dyemaking Table and Dyeing Barrel with stencils for stripes and checks. Shirts, sweaters, crop tops, skirts, jeans, capes, thigh highs, hats, chokers, and plushies. Its guidebook is the Weaver's Manual.

HatsSimple Hats

Nearly 300 hats. Monsters sometimes drop a Hat Grab-Bag (they're in chests too); right-click it for a random hat. Turn unwanted hats into scraps: 4 scraps make a new bag, and 2 common scraps trade up for 1 uncommon. Show them off on a Hat Display (a log on a slab).

Where clothes go

Hats, chokers, and other extras go in the Accessories slots: open your inventory and click the Accessories button next to your character. They don't use up your armor slots, so you can wear a hat and a helmet.

  • Sew a full trainer outfit
  • Spin, weave, and dye your own shirt
  • Open a hat grab-bag

Show Your Outfit

  1. Wear armor for protection, hide it for looks: pause menu → Mods → Show Me Your Skin! → settings. Hide any piece; you keep its protection. Each of you chooses your own.
  2. Hide pieces completely rather than making them see-through. Immersive Armors doesn't draw correctly when partly transparent.
  3. Your MCA look (from /mca editor with the model set to Villager) should show through hidden armor. See Villagers & Family.

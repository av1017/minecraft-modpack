The questions you'll actually have in your first week, with the fix and where to read more. The game usually tells you the reason too: watch the line above your hotbar.

Crafting

It says / you seeWhy, and the fix
"... belongs to an age your group hasn't reached"It's from a later age. Earn gym badges (Ages & Badges).
"You don't know how to make ... yet"Gear or a must-learn block. Do its quest (L), or ask someone who's mastered it for a lesson (Learning Recipes).
"You need an instruction book to make ..."Furniture or decor. Find its book in a Prize Crate (which book).
"Making ... takes Blacksmithing" (or another discipline)Learn the discipline (Disciplines).
"Still learning ...: you need more ingredients in the grid"Learners pay extras. Put more of each ingredient in the slots (how many).
"Use a crafting table to make ... while you're still learning it"The 2×2 grid has no room for extras; use a crafting table.
My sword came out "Crude"Quality follows your skill when you craft. Practice, or have a master craft it (Quality).
The station won't open ("You don't know Cooking yet...")It belongs to a discipline (Disciplines).

Gear

QuestionAnswer
Why is my new sword so weak?Skill starts at 0%, which is 35% strength. Use it: it climbs fast at first. Hover it to see your numbers (Skill).
I'm good with my iron pickaxe; why is my iron axe bad?Skill is per item. Each vanilla tool's quest asks for 60% with the same tool one tier down (the iron axe wants the stone axe); modded gear asks for the matching vanilla piece of its age (Quest Book).
What's a "Strange Object"?Something from an age your group hasn't reached. Carry it and use it to figure it out (Discovery).
My pickaxe dropped nothing from oreIt's a Strange Object to you, and you can't harvest with tools you don't understand yet.
Can I lose skill?No. Dying, waiting or switching gear never lowers it.
Why can't I enchant this?You need the Enchanting discipline and must have mastered the item (Enchanting).

The World

QuestionAnswer
The Nether portal doesn't take me anywhere"You can't go there until the Nether Age": 5 different gym badges. The End needs 8.
My elytra won't glideGliding waits for the End Age.
How do I sleep without wool?A leaf bed (Bushcraft). Leaves drop by hand 3 times in 10.
How do I see the group's progress?/ages age in chat.
Does this apply in Our World or our LAN worlds?No, only in Cobble Valley.

Lessons & Crates

It says / you seeWhy, and the fix
"You haven't mastered ..., so you can't teach it yet"Only masters (100%) teach.
"The lesson ended: you moved too far apart"Stay within 6 blocks until the bar fills.
"The lesson ended: the teacher put it away"The teacher has to keep holding the item.
"... already knows how to make ..."Nothing left to teach them for that item.
The book wasn't used upYou already knew everything in it. Pass it on.
"These instructions make no sense yet"The book is from a later age. Keep it for later.
My End crate gave an older bookThere are no End or Champion books, so those crates hand out books from the latest age that has them.

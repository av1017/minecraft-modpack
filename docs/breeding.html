Pokémon Eggs

Breed your Pokémon in a Pasture Block to get eggs, then carry the eggs around until they hatch. Babies can inherit regional forms and moves, sometimes get a hidden ability, and are more likely to be shiny when the parents come from two different trainers, so breed yours with your partner's.

Getting Eggs

  1. Place a Pasture Block, Cobblemon's own block for letting Pokémon roam. Find its recipe in the REI list: hover it and press Y.
  2. Put two Pokémon that can breed in it (see the next section).
  3. Turn breeding on with the breeding button that Cobbreeding adds to the pasture screen.
  4. Wait. A new egg shows up every 7 to 12 minutes while someone is nearby. The pasture holds 5 eggs; open it to collect them, or put a hopper under it to pull them out.
  • Get your first egg

Who Can Breed

The same rules as the main Pokémon games.

PairResult
A male and a female that share an egg groupAn egg of the mother's species, at its first evolution stage
Any breedable Pokémon + a DittoAn egg of the other Pokémon. Ditto is the only way to breed genderless Pokémon.
Ditto + DittoNothing
Legendaries, mythicals, and other "can't breed" PokémonNothing

A Pokémon's egg groups are on its Pokédex page. Babies can inherit regional forms (Alolan, Galarian, Hisuian, Paldean) and some looks, like Burmy's cloak or Magikarp's pattern.

Hatching

  1. Keep the egg in your inventory. It counts down while you play; hover it to check.
  2. Speed it up with a party Pokémon that has Flame Body, Magma Armor, or Steam Engine, like in the main games.
  3. When it's done, it hatches straight into your party. Leave a party slot free.
  • Hatch an egg
  • Get a Flame Body Pokémon (like Slugma or Litwick) for faster hatching

Every Key

Every key the mods add, in both worlds, and which ones overlap. The bindings come from each profile's settings file and from inside the mods themselves, so they're exactly what the game uses. Change any of them in Options → Controls → Key Binds.

Do any keys clash?

I checked every binding in both profiles, plus the default keys inside each new mod, for keys used twice. The sync fixes each real clash automatically: it moves the mod's key, but only if you haven't already changed it yourself.

FIXED BY THE SYNC · VEHICLES Ship, plane & bike buttons → C and X Small Ships, Immersive Aircraft, and PokeBike put their keys on R, I, O, B, and F, which already throw Pokémon, open your party, make waypoints, and swap your offhand. Now every vehicle uses C and X (see the table below). Each mod only reacts while you ride its own vehicle, so they never fire together.
FIXED BY THE SYNC Plane mouse steering: ` → Right Alt Backtick is the zoom key in this profile.
FIXED BY THE SYNC MCA skin library: U → F7 U is Xaero's waypoint list. Every letter is already taken in this profile, and the skin library is rarely needed.
WORKS WITH KEYBIND FIX Left Alt Riding free look / Portal Device close / plane free look Plain Minecraft lets only one action use a key; the others silently do nothing. That's why Alt+click on the Portal Device didn't work before. The Keybind Fix mod lets every action on a key work, and these three never overlap: one is for riding Pokémon, one is for clicking with the Portal Device, one is for flying.
FIXED BY THE SYNC R Throw Pokémon / reload shaders Iris put "reload shaders" on R, so every Pokémon throw also froze the game while shaders reloaded. Iris's R is now unbound in the Cobblemon world.
FIXED BY THE SYNC O Shader pack menu O is Cobblemon's "hide party" and 26.3's Friends key. Iris's O is unbound in both worlds; the menu is still at Options → Video Settings → Shader Packs.
FIXED BY THE SYNC Open backpack: B → G Traveler's Backpack defaults to B, which is Xaero's "new waypoint". In the Cobblemon world the backpack opens with G.
FIXED BY THE SYNC Backpack tool swap: Z → H Z is Xaero's "enlarge minimap". The backpack's tool/hose key is H in the Cobblemon world.
FIXED BY THE SYNC Portal gun menu: V → J V is the camera key in the Cobblemon world. The portal gun menu opens with J.
FIXED BY THE SYNC Show recipe: R → Y REI's "show recipe" on R also made the sorting mod sort your inventory. Recipes are on Y now, next to U for uses; R only sorts.
FIXED BY THE SYNC AutoSwitch on/off: R → N R throws your Pokémon. AutoSwitch also no longer swaps to flint and steel when you right-click a creeper.
FIXED BY THE SYNC Cinematic zoom: F6 → off F6 is First-person Model's toggle. Zoomify's second, cinematic zoom mode is left without a key.
WORKS AS-IS R Send out Pokémon / sort inventory Cobblemon uses R out in the world. Inventory Profiles Next uses R only while an inventory or chest is open. Different screens, so they never fire together.
WATCH OUT R+C Sorting settings Pressing R+C out in the world also sends out your Pokémon, since R goes first. Open sorting settings from inside your inventory instead, or through Mods on the pause menu.
FIXED BY THE SYNC · OUR WORLD Camera view: C → V C is the zoom key now, so Our World's camera view moved to V, the same as the Cobblemon world.
WORKS AS-IS · OUR WORLD B Open backpack Our World has no Xaero's maps, so the backpack keeps its default B. F3+B (show hitboxes) only fires while F3 is held.
No clash on M: Xaero's World Map uses M, and Cobblemon's Summary is on I, not M.
Red keys in Controls are expected. Options → Controls marks C, X, and Left Alt in red because several actions share them. Thanks to Keybind Fix they all work, each in its own situation. C and X are also Minecraft's creative "save/load hotbar" keys, which only do anything in Creative mode while you also press a number.

The sync installs these and switches them on the first time. Each player can turn them off or reorder them without affecting the other: texture packs never need to match for LAN.

Fresh AnimationsBoth worlds

Smooth, natural animations for vanilla mobs. Pokémon keep their own animations. Needs the EMF and ETF helper mods, which are installed.

Turning packs on, off, or reordering

  1. Options → Resource Packs.
  2. The right column is what's on. Packs higher in the list win when two change the same thing.
  3. Hover a pack and click the arrows to turn it off or move it, then Done.
If mobs look stiff or broken with Fresh Animations on, check that the Entity Model Features and Entity Texture Features mods show up under Mods in the pause menu. The sync installs both.

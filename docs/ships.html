Ships

Real sailing ships that catch the wind, carry cargo, and fire cannons. There are only seven ships, but each comes in 11 kinds of wood, so the creative menu shows 77 of them. The wood only changes the look. In survival you build them at a Dockyard.

Finding Them in Creative

We have so many mods that the creative menu has more than one page of tabs.

  1. Open the creative inventory and click the small arrows at the top right, next to the tabs, until you see the SmallShips Mod tab.
  2. Or use the Search tab (the compass) and type a ship's name: cog, brigg, galley, drakkar, galleon, caravel, or dhow.
  3. Or type @smallships in the REI item list on the right of any inventory to see everything the mod adds.

Old videos show a Rowing Boat and a War Galley. Those aren't in this version; the seven below are the whole fleet.

The Seven Ships

From the ship descriptions inside the mod. A tail wind blows from behind you, a side wind from the side, and a head wind into your face. The white lines on the water and the banner on the mast show which way it's blowing.

Galley1 mast + oars · 2 cannons · warm seas

Row at nearly full speed in any wind with the sail down; a side wind gives it a strong boost under sail. Can ram other ships without hurting its own hull.

Drakkar1 mast + oars · no cannons · cold seas

A Viking longship. Rows at about ¾ speed in any wind. Hang up to 10 shields along the sides to take less damage, and it breaks through ice.

Cog1 mast · 4 cannons · cold seas

A simple sturdy sailing ship. Fast with a tail wind, a little help from a side wind.

Dhow2 masts · 4 cannons · warm seas

Likes a side wind, and keeps full speed with a full cargo hold, so it's the trading ship.

Caravel2 masts · 4 cannons · any sea

The only ship that can sail into the wind: head and side winds both speed it up. Same speed in every biome.

Brigg2 masts · 6 cannons · cold seas

A bigger warship. Strong tail-wind boost, moderate side-wind boost.

Galleon3 masts · 10 cannons · any sea

The flagship. Keeps full speed even with all 10 cannons mounted. Strong tail-wind boost; same speed in every biome.

Building One (Survival)

Ships aren't crafted in a crafting table. You craft a Dockyard, place it by the water, and it builds the ship for you.

  1. Place the Dockyard next to open water that's deep enough. If it isn't, the Dockyard tells you how deep the ship needs.
  2. Open it, go to the Build tab, and pick the ship and the wood.
  3. Bring the materials from the table below. The Dockyard lists anything you're missing.
  4. Click Build ship. It works for a few minutes, then the ship appears on the water.

Upgrades, Cannons & Style

Sail within 16 blocks of a Dockyard and open its Modify tab. From there you can add upgrades, mount cannons, repair the hull and sails, dye the sails, hang banners, name your ship, and hang shields on a Drakkar.

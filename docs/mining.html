BASIC

Get Diamonds Reliably

The floor for "mining actually works." No fancy setup, minimal risk, still finds diamonds consistently.

  • Dig a main shaft down to Y −54 or Y −59 using a ladder/staircase or a straight vertical shaft with water at the bottom to break your fall.
  • Branch mine: dig a central tunnel, then 1×2 side tunnels every 2 blocks. This is the current community-recommended default — it exposes more new block faces per block mined than old-style strip mining. (wiki, 2026 comparison)
  • Carry a water bucket at all times below Y −40 — for extinguishing yourself, redirecting lava, and safe falls.
  • Carry blocks to wall off any cave opening, lava pool, or mob you break into — sealing first, looting second.
  • An iron pickaxe is the minimum for diamond ore — stone won't cut it, and neither will wood or gold.
INTERMEDIATE

Mine With the Math

Once basic branch mining feels slow, these are the actual numbers behind why spacing and enchants matter — not just "trust me."

Tighter spacing = nothing missed but more swings. Wider spacing = better ore-per-swing but some veins between tunnels go unseen.

2-BLOCK SPACINGFull coverage
No vein missed, but exposes far more stone/deepslate per diamond found. Best if you have time, not patience for gaps.
6-BLOCK SPACING~1.7% hit rate
Mathematically the best ore-per-block-mined ratio — tunnels never re-cover ground. Best if you value speed over completeness.
Community math consensus: 6-block spacing is the efficiency peak per swing, 2-block spacing is the completeness peak. Most players split the difference around 3–4 blocks.
Source: Minecraft Forum efficiency math thread
  • Pick your branch spacing deliberately — 2 blocks for max coverage, 6 for max ore-per-swing, 3–4 as the common middle ground.
  • Fortune III vs Silk Touch: for diamonds, Fortune III averages a bit over 2x the drops per ore. Silk Touch is only better when you want to move/store the ore block itself. Don't put both on one pickaxe — carry two if you need both effects. (source)
  • Cave-diving can beat branch mining in ore-per-minute if you find a large post-1.18 cave system — but avoid relying on fully open caves for diamond specifically: air-exposed veins are more likely to be partially cut off by generation than veins found underground intact.
  • Run Efficiency V on your mining pickaxe once available — it meaningfully cuts time-per-block, which compounds over a long mining session more than most players expect.
EXPERT / ABOVE & BEYOND

Blast Mining & Risk Trade-offs

Faster than branch mining, but each technique here trades a resource cost or a real risk for speed. Know the trade before you commit to it.

  • TNT mining (Overworld): clears large volumes fast, but roughly 30% of blown-up ore blocks are destroyed rather than dropping — including diamonds. Community consensus treats this as a high-risk/high-reward bulk-clearing tool, not a diamond-hunting method — the destroyed-block rate cancels out most of the time saved when diamonds are the actual goal. (community discussion)
  • TNT duping (piston + slime block setup) is a long-standing, unpatched glitch mostly used for large-scale terrain clearing (tree farms, world-eaters) rather than personal ore stock — it doesn't put TNT in your inventory, it primes and drops existing TNT. Treat as a build-project tool, not a mining shortcut. (source)
  • Know before you use it: TNT-based mining is a grey area on many multiplayer servers even where technically not a bannable exploit — check server rules before using it beyond singleplayer.
EXPERT

Ancient Debris & the Bed-Blast Method

Ancient debris has the highest blast resistance of any minable Nether block — which is exactly why explosives outperform pickaxes here, if you accept the risk.

  • Fortune and Silk Touch do nothing on ancient debris — it always drops exactly 1 block per block mined regardless of enchant. Don't waste a pickaxe slot on it for this purpose; Efficiency + Unbreaking + Mending is the actual optimal ancient debris pickaxe. (source)
  • Standard method: branch mine at Y 15 where the two generation bands overlap — normal pickaxe mining, safer, slower.
  • Bed-blast method (expert): dig tunnels at Y 11–15 ending in a bed; shelter behind a block, then use the bed to detonate it (beds explode outside the Overworld). The blast reveals debris around the explosion point without destroying it, since debris survives almost anything short of direct pickaxe contact needs.
  • Bring multiple Fire Resistance potions before attempting bed-blast mining — accidentally exposing or stepping into lava mid-blast is the single most common way this method goes wrong. (source: Minecraft Wiki)
  • Mine lower (closer to Y 8–11) rather than right at the Y 15 peak if you want to reduce the odds of breaking into an open lava ocean, which are more common near the upper part of the debris band.

STAGE 1

Early Game — Survive & Stabilize

Don't move on from this stage until you can survive a night, mine underground safely, and aren't hungry. See The Diamond Depths and The Farm Stack for the deep dives on these.

Gear Milestones
  • Stone tools + furnace
  • Iron pickaxe, sword, and a shield — this is the single most important early upgrade
  • Full iron armor set
  • Bed + safe, lit shelter
Infrastructure
  • Basic food farm running
  • Sorted storage system
  • First iron golem farm
Places to Find
  • A village (plains/desert/savanna biomes first)
  • A cave system worth branch mining near
STAGE 2

Mid Game — Gear Up & Push Underground

Diamonds, enchanting, and your first Nether trip. See The Rush to Mending and Reading the Dark for the deep dives.

Gear Milestones
  • Diamond pickaxe, sword, armor
  • Mending on your main tools (villager trade-reset)
  • Enchanting table + bookshelves, Efficiency/Unbreaking/Protection
  • Flint and steel + obsidian for a Nether portal
Infrastructure
  • Sugar cane farm (feeds paper → emeralds)
  • Villager trading hall + breeder
  • Mob XP farm
Places to Explore
  • A Nether Fortress — 10 blaze rods is the real target
  • A Bastion Remnant — piglin bartering, gold armor for safety
  • A Trial Chamber — see the callout below, genuinely worth a detour
Trial Chambers generate between Y −40 and −20 and are one of the newer structures worth prioritizing: clear a trial spawner's waves for a trial key, or drink an Ominous Bottle (from raid captains or vaults) to convert nearby trial spawners into Ominous versions mid-run — these drop Ominous Trial Keys, which unlock Ominous Vaults holding genuinely unique loot, including the Heavy Core and Flow Armor Trim, unmatched anywhere else in the game. Source: Minecraft Wiki, Ominous Vault
STAGE 3

Late Game — Boss Fights

Minecraft has exactly two official bosses with a health bar, plus one unofficial third that's arguably scarier than both.

🐉 The Ender Dragon 200 HP × 100 (i.e. 100 hearts worth) Requires: stronghold found via Eyes of Ender, full enchanted gear, blocks for the crystal towers. See the End fight strategy — pop the crystals first, then fight or bed-trick the dragon.
💀 The Wither 300 HP × 150 Summoned by the player: 4 Soul Sand/Soil in a T-shape topped with 3 Wither Skeleton skulls. Build the summon structure underground or behind blast-resistant walls — it explodes on spawn. Drops a Nether Star, used to craft a Beacon.
👁️ The Warden (unofficial) 500 HP, up to 6 hearts of damage per hit Found in the Deep Dark. Not meant to be fought — see Reading the Dark for how to avoid triggering one entirely.
Places to Explore
  • Woodland Mansion (Dark Forest biome) — rare, often thousands of blocks from spawn, Totems of Undying
  • Ocean Monument (deep ocean) — guardian farming, sponges, prismarine
  • Ancient City (Deep Dark, below Y 0) — best vanilla loot outside the End, guarded by Warden risk
Gear Milestones
  • Netherite upgrade on your full kit
  • A Beacon (post-Wither) for area buffs
STAGE 4

Post-Game — Whatever "Done" Means to You

The credits roll after the dragon, but the world keeps going. These are the goals most players actually chase afterward.

Gear & Items
  • Elytra (from an End City, found via End Ships past the main island)
  • Shulker boxes — portable, stackable storage that survives inventory death
  • A dragon egg (trophy, no functional use)
Open-Ended Projects
  • A megabase or full villager city
  • Redstone contraptions purely for fun — see the Redstone page once it's built
  • A second world/server for you and your girlfriend to build in together

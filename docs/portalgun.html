Portal Gun

The portal gun from Rick and Morty. Set a destination, fire at a wall or the floor, and a green portal opens there, with its twin at the destination. Step through before it closes. It's a late-game build: the gun needs an End Crystal, which needs a Ghast Tear from the Nether and an Eye of Ender.

Building It

Only the workbench uses the normal crafting table. Everything after it is made in the Portal Gun Workbench: put the ingredients in, with no pattern needed, and take the result.

Then, in the workbench, in this order

  1. Circuit Board ×3: each one is slime ball comparator 3 gold nuggets 2 repeaters
  2. Bootleg Portal Fluid: Potion of Poison ender pearl. It's an ingredient only; never fuel the gun with it (see Dangers).
  3. Quantum Leap Elixir: amethyst shard honey bottle blaze powder
  4. Portal Fluid: Bootleg Portal Fluid Quantum Leap Elixir
  5. End Crystal (normal crafting table): 7 glass around an Eye of Ender (ender pearl + blaze powder), with a ghast tear below it.
  6. Portal Gun: End Crystal Portal Fluid 2 iron ingots 3 Circuit Boards. It comes with a full tank.
Make extra Portal Fluid while you're at it. Each bottle is another 64 portals, and the Fuel upgrade needs 4 of them.
  • Place a Portal Gun Workbench
  • Make 3 Circuit Boards
  • Make Portal Fluid
  • Craft an End Crystal
  • Craft the Portal Gun

Using It

Do thisHow
Set the destinationHold the gun and press J. (The mod's default is V; the sync moved it because V is the camera key in this world.) Type X, Y, Z in Coordinate Travel. A box left empty uses where you're standing. Randomize picks a random spot within 10,000 blocks and finds a safe height.
FireRight-click with the gun in your main hand. A portal opens where you're aiming, and its twin opens at the destination.
Come backStep back through the twin portal before it closes (10 seconds by default), or fire a new portal with your old coordinates. Note your coordinates with F3 before you leave, or save them as a waypoint.
RefuelGun in your main hand, Portal Fluid in your off hand (F swaps an item there), then right-click. One bottle fills the tank. Hover over the gun to see its fuel.
Apply an upgradeSame trick: upgrade in your off hand, right-click with the gun. (Or add it through the gun's settings menu.)
Bring a friendBoth of you can walk through the same open portal, so one shot moves you both. Some mobs are blocked from portals by the mod's config.
Other dimensions need the Dimension upgrade. Without it, the menu only shows coordinates, and portals stay in the dimension you're in.
  • Portal somewhere and back
  • Refuel with a Portal Fluid bottle in your off hand
  • Take both players through the same portal

Waypoints

With the Waypoints upgrade, open the gun's menu (J), choose Waypoints, and save where you're standing: home, the Healing Machine, a gym. Pick a saved one to fire a portal straight there.

  1. Make Data Cards in the workbench: Disc Template book & quill gold nugget. A Data Card holds waypoints without a gun.
  2. Share waypoints between guns: put a gun or Data Card in the workbench and choose Edit Portal Gun Waypoints. You can copy or move waypoints to the other device, or delete them.
  3. Copy your list to the other player's gun so you both have the same destinations.
  • Save a waypoint at home
  • Copy waypoints to the other player's gun with a Data Card

Dispenser & Barrier

BlockWhat it does
Portal DispenserOpens a portal when it gets a redstone signal, so you can wire a button to a fixed destination. Holds 16 fuel. Its recipe uses up a whole portal gun, so build it only once you can spare one.
Sub-Ether BarrierNeeds redstone power. Within 3 chunks, it blocks any portal that doesn't carry its code. Set your gun's code in Security Settings to get through.

Dangers

Never fuel the gun with Bootleg Portal Fluid. It fills the tank, but per the mod's wiki, portals made with it kill anyone who touches them ("incinerated by a bootleg portal"). Use it only to make real Portal Fluid.
SettingWhat to know
Self destructIf you turn it on, a button appears on the coordinates screen. Pressing it drops the gun, which can't be picked up, and it explodes 10 seconds later. Leave this off.
Biometric lockOnly the owner can use the gun. Whoever fires it first becomes the owner.
Transfer ownershipHands the gun to another player. If the lock is on, you lose access to it.
Portaling blindRandomize finds a safe height, but typed coordinates don't. Check the Y value so you don't land inside stone or in the sky.

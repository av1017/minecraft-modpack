Portal Device

A handheld device that opens a linked blue and orange portal pair on any two solid surfaces. Walk, fall, or fling through; your speed carries over, mounts come with you, and each player has their own pair. Built for this modpack, so it works with everything else in the Cobblemon world.

Getting One

Craft it at a normal crafting table, find it in the creative Tools & Utilities tab, or search "Portal Device" in the REI item list (hover it and press Y for the recipe).

  • Craft a Portal Device (ender pearls come from Endermen)

Controls

Do thisWhat happens
Left-clickOpen your blue portal where you aim. Shooting again moves it.
Right-clickOpen your orange portal.
Hold Left Alt + clickClose just that color: left-click closes blue, right-click closes orange.
Sneak + right-clickClose both of your portals.

Change the close key in Options → Controls → Key Binds → Twin Portals. It isn't Left Ctrl by default because Ctrl is the sprint key, and holding sprint while shooting would close portals instead of opening them.

A portal looks dimmer until its partner is open. Bright means it's linked and ready.

Where Portals Go

Walls get an upright portal 1 block wide and 2 tall. Floors and ceilings get one lying flat, pointing the way you face. If the exact spot doesn't fit but the column next to it does, the portal snaps over.

Works onDoesn't work on
Full, solid, non-see-through blocks: stone, dirt, planks, bricks, snow blocks, packed or blue ice, sand…Glass, regular ice, leaves, snow layers, slabs, stairs, fences: anything without a full solid face

If it won't place, the message says why

MessageMeaning
"Too far away: portals reach 128 blocks here"Past your reach. Reach is your render distance × 16 (up to 192). The far landscape from Distant Horizons isn't real blocks yet, so it can't hold a portal.
"That surface won't hold a portal"The block isn't a full solid face (see above).
"Not enough room for a portal there"Something is in front of the wall, or another portal is in the way.
  • Open a blue and an orange portal and walk through

Going Through

WhatHow it works
MomentumYour speed and direction carry through. Jump from high up into a floor portal and you shoot out of a wall portal.
Out of a wallYou face out of the wall, the way you'd expect from the game Portal.
Out of a floorYou keep facing the way you were, looking level. A slow exit gives a little hop to the side so you don't drop straight back in.
Out of a ceilingYou keep your heading and look a little down, toward where you'll land.
RidingYou and your mount (horse, ridden Pokémon, boat, minecart) go through together and stay mounted.
Items, animals, PokémonGo through like you do. Anyone can use anyone's portals.
Hostile mobsCan't pass: zombies, creepers, skeletons, spiders and the rest bounce off, so nothing follows you home.
Other dimensionsA pair can link the Overworld and the Nether or End.
  • Do a fling: fall into a floor portal, fly out of a wall
  • Ride a horse or Pokémon through a portal

How Long They Last

  1. Portals stay until you remove them, no matter how far you walk. They're saved with the world, so logging out doesn't close them.
  2. They close by themselves when you shoot a new one of the same color, close them yourself, the block behind breaks, or the space in front gets blocked.
  3. After 15 minutes with nothing going through a pair, it closes, and the owner gets a message. Only time the world is running counts. Using either portal resets both.
  4. A portal only works while someone is near its entrance (like farms). The exit can be anywhere.
Change the timeout with /gamerule twinportalsIdleMinutes 30, or 0 to keep portals open forever. Commands need cheats: in an existing world, use Esc → Open to LAN → Allow Cheats, then run it.

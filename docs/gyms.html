The Level Cap

The rule everything else follows. Each player has their own cap.

  1. Pokémon in your party at or above your cap gain no experience. They don't lose levels; they just stop growing.
  2. Trainers won't battle you if your party has a Pokémon above their cap. Keep stronger Pokémon in the PC when you go trainer hunting.
  3. Beat your series' next key trainer (usually a gym leader) to raise the cap. The new cap matches that series' next big battle.
  4. Wild trainers scale to your team but never go above your cap.
Until you pick a series, you're in "No Series" with a cap of 15 that never rises. Picking a series is step one. Our Pokémon from before the mod are probably above 15, which is why trainers may refuse to fight at first.

Getting Started

  1. Craft a Trainer Card (above). Each player needs their own copy, but it stores no data: it shows the progress of whoever holds it.
  2. Find the Trainer Association. This NPC spawns near players who carry a Trainer Card and haven't started a series, and near villages (3 or more beds). It despawns when its chunk unloads, so trade straight away. A name tag or a boat keeps it around.
  3. Trade for a series. Right-click the Association to open its trade screen. Each series is one trade, and it gives the trade item back. Your cap jumps to fit that series' first leader.
  4. Read your Trainer Card. Defeated trainers are listed with their whole team. The next key trainer shows as ???, but you can see which biomes they spawn in.

Choosing a Series

A series is a story path: 8 leaders, rivals, an evil team, the Elite Four, and a champion, each raising your cap. Only your series' trainers show on your card. Each player picks their own.

SeriesDifficultyWhat it is
Brilliant Diamond / Shining Pearl5 / 10"A casual series but still with decent difficulty." Sinnoh's leaders. They spawn out in the world; the gym buildings below are for Kanto, not Sinnoh.
Radical Red9 / 10"A difficult series taking place in the Kanto region." These are the leaders the gym buildings are made for. Expect tough, competitive teams.
Unbound8 / 10The Borrius region. Unlocks after completing another series.
Freeroam—Pauses your series with a cap of 100. Unlocks after completing a series. Switch back anytime; your cap returns to where it was.
Which one? Radical Red if you want the gym buildings to mean something and don't mind a hard fight. BDSP for a relaxed first run. Finishing any series raises your luck, which improves trainer loot for good.

Finding Them

  1. Your Trainer Card glows when your next key trainer is loaded in the world, and a holographic arrow points to them. If the arrow spins, they're in another dimension.
  2. Head for the right biome using the table above, past the edge of the explored map. A horse, boat, or riding Pokémon helps.
  3. Mark each gym with a map waypoint (B) when you find it.
  4. Portal gun shortcut: the Structure Locator upgrade should find gyms, since they're normal structures. See Portal Gun.
  5. With cheats on (Open to LAN → Allow Cheats): /locate structure rgs:pewter_gym. Swap in cerulean_gym, kanto_league, and so on.

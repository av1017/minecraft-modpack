PHASE 0

Day 1–2 Setup

Get off wood tools and into a safe, lit shelter before the first proper night.

  • Punch trees for wood → craft a crafting table and wooden pickaxe, axe, sword (or skip straight to stone if rock is exposed nearby).
  • Mine at least 11 cobblestone → stone pickaxe, axe, sword + a furnace.
  • Grab surface coal for torches — lighting your base stops mob spawns.
  • Shear or kill sheep for wool → craft a bed. Sleeping skips night and sets spawn.
  • Dig in or find shelter before nightfall — a hillside hole with a dirt wall is fine, don't overbuild yet.
  • Stockpile a little extra wood — always keep enough on hand for a quick tool replacement.
Goal by end of Day 2: stone tools, furnace, bed, safe shelter.
New in 26.3: the straw bed (straw + wool) skips the night without resetting your spawn point — but it's single-use and can't be used in the Nether or End. Handy for exploring away from your real bed; doesn't replace it.
PHASE 1

Early Game — Iron & Infrastructure

Iron gear, a food loop, and organized storage so you stop wasting time on logistics.

  • Mine and smelt iron ore → iron pickaxe (needed for diamond/redstone), iron sword, and at least an iron chestplate.
  • Craft a shield (iron ingot + plank) — cuts early damage drastically, especially from skeletons.
  • Dig gravel for flint → craft flint and steel for the Nether portal later.
  • Till dirt near water, plant a wheat plot (break tall grass for seeds).
  • Pen a few animals: cows (leather + beef), pigs, chickens (eggs → more chickens).
  • Set up sorted storage — chests by category: ores, food, building blocks, tools.

Branch mine Y −58 2-block tunnel spacing Fortune III > Silk Touch for yield
  • Branch mining (current best): dig a main tunnel at Y −58/−59, then 1×2 branch tunnels off it spaced exactly 2 blocks apart. This exposes more new block faces per block broken than old-style strip mining, which is why it's overtaken strip mining as the standard ore-hunting method.
  • Strip mining (solid, gapless tunnels) is now mainly for clearing bulk stone or flattening a build area, not for ore-hunting — branch mining finds more ore per minute.
  • Cave diving: post-1.18 caves are huge and ore-dense — can outpace branch mining if you find a good system, but yields are less predictable. Wall off lava and mob spawns as you go, and avoid fully open caves for diamond specifically — air-exposed veins often get partially discarded by world generation.
  • Always carry torches, blocks to wall off danger, food, a water bucket (redirects lava, breaks falls), and a spare pickaxe.
PHASE 3

Enchanting & Villager Trading

One good enchanting setup plus a trading hall covers nearly everything you need.

  • Build an enchanting table (diamond + obsidian — get obsidian by pouring water on lava, then mining with a diamond pickaxe).
  • Surround it with 15 bookshelves, one block gap from the table, for level 30 max enchants.
  • Farm XP through mining, mob kills, and breeding — a simple mob grinder speeds this up a lot.
  • Find a village and trade-reset librarians (break + replace their lectern) to reroll for specific enchanted books, including ones the table can't give you.

Mending only comes from villager trades — get it early on your main pickaxe, sword, and armor so gear repairs itself with XP.

PHASE 5

The Nether

Blaze rods and ender pearls are the two things you're actually here for.

  • Build the portal: 10 obsidian minimum, 4×5 frame, light with flint and steel.
  • Bring: full iron+ armor, sword, bow and arrows, blocks for ledges, and at least one piece of gold armor — piglins stay neutral around any gold.
  • Raid a Nether fortress for blaze rods (needed for brewing stands and Eyes of Ender).
  • Get ender pearls via piglin bartering (safer) or Endermen kills (riskier) → craft Eyes of Ender — aim for 12–16, some break in flight.
  • Optional: mine ancient debris at Y 15, smelt to scrap, combine 4 scrap + 4 gold ingots → netherite ingot, upgrade diamond gear at a smithing table.
Remember: the Nether is 1:8 scale to the Overworld — mark your portal with torches or blocks so you can find your way back.
PHASE 6

Finding the Stronghold

Throw, follow, repeat — then gear up before you step through.

  • Throw an Eye of Ender, follow the direction it flies, repeat until one sinks down near a spot — the stronghold is roughly beneath it.
  • Dig down or explore to find the End Portal room, and fill the frame's empty slots with your remaining Eyes.
  • Full enchanted armor and weapons (Protection IV, Mending, Unbreaking) — this is not the place to go in half-geared.
  • Bring at least a stack of blocks (for the crystal towers), golden apples, food, a spare pickaxe, and a bed.
PHASE 7

The End Fight

Crystals first, dragon second — the order is what makes this fight fast instead of brutal.

  • Snipe the Ender Crystals on the obsidian towers first — they continuously heal the dragon. Bow from range, or melee if towers are caged in iron bars.
  • Once crystals are down, focus the dragon while it perches on the exit portal — your best damage window.
  • Sidestep the dive-bomb charge — don't try to tank it.
  • Bed trick: place and use a bed near where the dragon perches — beds explode in the End, dealing a big burst of damage.
  • Grab the dragon egg (trophy only) and jump into the exit portal for the credits.
The game keeps going after the credits — that's just the "beat the game" marker. Elytra from End Cities, shulker boxes, and totems of undying are the natural next goals.

⚡ Fast-Track Cheat Sheet

If you just want the condensed version, this is the whole run.

  1. Wood → stone tools → iron tools, armor, and a shield.
  2. Branch mine at Y −58 for diamond + redstone, grabbing iron/coal along the way.
  3. Diamond pickaxe → obsidian → enchanting table + 15 bookshelves.
  4. Basic food farm + iron source so logistics stop costing you time.
  5. Nether run: blaze rods from a fortress, ender pearls from piglins or Endermen.
  6. Eyes of Ender → find the stronghold → gear up fully before entering the portal.
  7. In the End: pop the crystals first, then fight or bed-trick the dragon.

Gear by Age

Every tool, weapon, armor piece and shield in the pack, sorted by the age it belongs to, with what its mastery quest asks for. For stats and which gear is best, see Armor, Weapons & Style.

Materialsdecide the age: copper Stone, iron and steel Iron
60%skill with the tier below unlocks a quest
Blacksmithingfor smithing templates

How Our Armor & Weapon Mods Fit

Epic Knights400+ weapons, armor, shields

Each weapon comes in wood, stone, copper, iron, gold, diamond and netherite, so it sorts itself by tier. Steel is made by blasting iron, so steel gear is Iron Age. Bronze, tin and silver have no source in our pack; if they ever turn up they're Stone (tin, bronze) and Iron (silver).

Immersive Armorssets with powers

Follow their materials: wooden and bone sets are early, warrior, heavy, steampunk and slime Iron, prismarine and divine Diamond, robe (blaze powder) and wither Nether.

Cobblemon Armorsvillain-team looks

Mostly leather-tier, placed by their recipes (one needing coal lands in the Stone Age).

Trims & templatesBlacksmithing

Smithing templates and the smithing table need Blacksmithing (Iron Age). Cobbled Armor Trims' gem trims work the same way.

Clothes & hatsnot gear

Poke Clothing outfits and Weaver's Paradise clothes need Tailoring; they're cosmetic, so no skill. Simple Hats are cosmetic too.

Every Piece

Tap a mod to open its list. "Mastery quest needs" is what the (hidden or visible) quest asks for; "(or similar)" means any matching item works.

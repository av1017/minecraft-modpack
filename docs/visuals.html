Trying Another Base Pack

Faithful 32x is on now. Yuushya and Ashen are installed and switched off, ready to compare. Only switch one base pack on at a time.

  1. Options → Resource Packs. The right column is what's on.
  2. Turn Faithful off: hover it and click the left arrow.
  3. Turn the new base pack on: hover it in the left column and click the right arrow. It lands at the top of the list.
  4. Move it to the bottom. Hover it and click the down arrow until it sits below Better Leaves and Fast Better Grass. Left on top, it would cover up the 3D and foliage packs.
  5. Click Done and wait for the reload. Look at grass, leaves, stone, and a village before deciding.
Some packs are on Avery's PC only: Clarity, Equanimity, and John Smith Legacy (all 32x) are installed and switched off in Avery's Cobblemon profile for a side-by-side test. Whichever base pack wins goes into the modpack, and the sync then puts it on both PCs.

Shaders

The Iris mod runs shader packs; ours is Complementary Reimagined. It adds real-time shadows, warm sunlight, water reflections, and fog. Both worlds have it, and the sync leaves shaders exactly how you set them.

Do thisHow
Shaders on/offPress K, in either world. Handy for comparing, or for a quick frame-rate boost.
Choose a shader packOptions → Video Settings → Shader Packs. Its O key shortcut is turned off in both worlds, since O is Cobblemon's "hide party" and 26.3's Friends key.
Shader settingsOn that same screen, select Complementary and click Shader Pack Settings. The Profile at the top sets everything at once.
Reload shadersOnly needed after editing shader files. Its R key shortcut is turned off in the Cobblemon world because R throws your Pokémon, and every throw froze the game for a moment.
If it's laggy: plug the laptop in first, since Windows slows the graphics chip on battery. Then lower Complementary's Profile one step, or lower the render distance in Video Settings. Shadows and render distance cost the most.
  • Press K twice to see the world with and without shaders
  • Find a Complementary Profile that runs smoothly on your PC

Skies

Dramatic Skys paints custom skies: sunsets, clouds, and starry nights. The free demo includes a few of them. It needs the Skyboxify mod, which both worlds have.

Shaders draw their own sky. With Complementary on, you'll mostly see Complementary's sky, not Dramatic Skys. Press K at dusk or night to compare the two and keep whichever you like.
  • Look at a sunset with shaders off (K) to see Dramatic Skys

Distant Horizons

Shows land far past your normal render distance as simplified terrain, so mountains and coastlines stay visible on the horizon. Both worlds have it. It only changes what you see: the world itself is unchanged.

  1. The first visit is busy. It builds the far terrain in the background as you play, so expect more fan noise and a slow fill-in at first. It's saved after that, so returning to an area is fast.
  2. Settings: pause menu → Mods → Distant Horizons. The sync sets a light profile: 128 chunks in the Cobblemon world (48 in Our World), surface-only generation, low quality, and Distant Horizons' own shading and clouds off.
  3. It works with Complementary. The shader has its own settings for blending the far terrain into the fog.
This is the biggest performance cost in the pack. At its default 256 chunks with full detail, it maxed out the host laptop's graphics even with shaders off; with the light profile it runs smoothly with shaders on. If things get choppy, lower its distance (48 → 32) before touching anything else, and leave its SSAO, clouds, and finest detail off.
Playing on LAN: the player who joins builds far terrain only from land they've loaded themselves, so their horizon fills in more slowly than the host's.
  • Climb a hill and check the horizon
  • Set the draw distance so the game stays smooth

String, Solved

The reason we installed these mods. You now have four ways to get string, plus two recipes that remove the need for it.

WOOL TWEAKS1 wool → 4 stringAny color. The fastest option if you have sheep.
RUSTIC DELIGHT1 cotton boll → 1 stringFarmable. Slower per craft, but it's a renewable crop.
VANILLASpiders & cobwebsStill work. Spiders drop 0–2; cobwebs drop 1 when cut with a sword.
FARMER'S DELIGHT5 straw → 1 leadA lead without string or slime. Solves our earlier horse problem.
Books without leather: Farmer's Delight adds a book recipe using 3 paper + 1 canvas. Between cotton paper and cotton canvas, you can now make all 45 books for a full enchanting setup without hunting a single cow. See The Rush to Mending for the bookshelf layout.
WOOL TWEAKS

Wool Tweaks

Small mod, three changes. Nothing to build or find, so it works as soon as you load in.

RUSTIC DELIGHT

Rustic Delight

An add-on for Farmer's Delight. Cotton is the headline for us, but it also adds coffee, bell peppers, pancakes, calamari, and fried foods.

Starting a cotton farm

  1. Travel into new, unexplored forest. Wild Cotton only generates in forest biomes.
  2. Harvest the Wild Cotton. For the most out of each plant, put it on a Cutting Board and use a knife on it: you get Cotton Seeds plus a chance at a bonus drop.
  3. Plant the seeds on farmland like wheat. Water within 4 blocks and light keep it growing.
  4. Harvest the grown crop for Cotton Bolls, and replant the seeds it drops.
  5. Craft the bolls into what you need: string (1 → 1), paper (3 → 3), or canvas (4 → 1).

FARMER'S DELIGHT

Farmer's Delight

The biggest of the three. It's a full cooking and farming expansion: new crops, a kitchen, better soil, and meals that give real buffs. It came along because Rustic Delight requires it, but it's worth using on its own.

Built-in guide: press L in-game to open Advancements. Farmer's Delight lays out its progression there, so it doubles as a checklist. Source: Farmer's Delight Refabricated

Starter path: in this order

  1. Flint Knife (flint over a stick). Knives can't be made from wood or stone; flint is the cheapest. Break tall grass with it for Straw, and kill animals with it for guaranteed leather and feathers.
  2. Cutting Board. Place an item on it, then right-click with the right tool to break it into ingredients. Knives cut food, axes strip bark, pickaxes split bricks, shovels get clay from clay blocks.
  3. Stove. A campfire upgrade that holds 6 items and never needs fuel. It's also the heat source for the next two.
  4. Cooking Pot on top of the stove (a campfire, fire, or lava also works). Up to 6 ingredients in, and it can hold 64 servings. Most meals need a bowl to serve.
  5. Skillet. Cooks items in about 6 seconds, roughly 5× faster than a campfire. Use it in your hand near a heat source, or place it on the stove. It's also a heavy weapon with double knockback.

Meal buffs

COMFORT From meals served in a bowl

Your health keeps regenerating even when you're too hungry to heal normally. Shown as a silver shimmer on your hearts.

NOURISHMENT From meals served on a plate (feasts)

Running, jumping, and fighting stop draining hunger, so you go much longer without eating. It pauses only while you're healing. It also cancels the Hunger effect.

Feasts (Roast Chicken, Stuffed Pumpkin, Honey Glazed Ham, Shepherd's Pie) are placed as a block, and each one serves 4 bowls. Right-click the placed feast with a bowl to get a serving. That's handy for two players sharing a base.

Inventory Sorting

Three mods that make chests and your inventory faster to manage. All three run only on your PC, so your girlfriend doesn't need them for LAN, though she'll probably want them too.

Mod Menu: settings for everything

Adds a Mods button to the title screen and pause menu. It lists every installed mod, and clicking one opens its settings if it has any. Use it to change Inventory Profiles Next's hotkeys.

  • Open a chest and click a sort button.
  • Lock your hotbar tool slots so sorting skips them.
  • Try Shift + left-click drag to empty a row into a chest.
  • Open Mods from the pause menu and look through the settings.
LIBRARIES

The Helper Mods

These add nothing you'll see in-game. Some are libraries the mods above need; the rest make the game run faster. Don't delete them from the mods folder. The sync also gives this profile 4 GB of memory, instead of the launcher's default 2 GB.

Fabric API
Shared toolkit most Fabric mods are built on.
Cloth Config
Settings screens. Required by Rustic Delight.
Collective
Shared code for Serilum's mods. Required by Wool Tweaks.
libIPN
Required by Inventory Profiles Next.
Fabric Language Kotlin
Lets mods written in Kotlin run. Required by Inventory Profiles Next.
Placeholder API
Required by Mod Menu.
Sodium
Faster rendering: higher, steadier framerate. Runs on your PC only.
Lithium
Speeds up the world simulation. Helps most on the PC hosting the LAN world.
FerriteCore
Cuts how much memory Minecraft needs.
Playing together: for LAN to connect, your girlfriend's PC needs the three gameplay mods plus Fabric API, Cloth Config, and Collective. The inventory mods are optional for her. The full list and file names are in minecraftServer/MODS.md.

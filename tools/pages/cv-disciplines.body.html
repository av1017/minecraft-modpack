Disciplines

A discipline is a whole craft: Cooking, Tailoring, Blacksmithing, Enchanting, Brewing. Until you know it, its stations won't open for you and its recipes won't come out. Learn it once, by quest or from someone who has, and it's yours.

Learning One

  • Quest: each discipline has a quest in the Cobble Valley tab under its age. Gather what it lists and you've learned it, plus a Prize Crate.
  • Lesson: someone who knows the discipline holds its tool (the "teach it by holding" column), crouches and right-clicks you, and you both stay close until the bar fills. How lessons work
Trying a locked station tells you what you're missing: "You don't know Cooking yet, so you can't use the Cooking Pot." Crouch-clicking with a block in hand still places blocks against it.

Good to Know

  • Enchanting also needs the gear to be mastered (100%) before you can enchant it, at the table or with books on an anvil. Renaming and the grindstone always work. Enchanting
  • Blacksmithing covers the smithing table (netherite upgrades, armor trims) and crafting smithing templates.
  • Tailoring covers Weaver's Paradise's clothcrafting station and Poke Clothing's trainer outfits.
  • Cooking covers Farmer's Delight's cooking pot, skillet and cutting board. Plain furnace and campfire cooking needs nothing.
  • Brewing covers the brewing stand. (A hopper can still feed a stand someone else set up.)

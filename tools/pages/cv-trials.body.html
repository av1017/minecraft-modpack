The Keeper's Trials

Ada Thornwood's Puzzle Temple: six sealed chambers that only the Portal Device can solve. Portals stick to the white panels and never to the dark ones, so every chamber is about which white panels you can see, and how fast you're moving when you go through.

The Rules

  • White panels take portals. Dark panels refuse them (needs Twin Portals 0.5 or newer).
  • The temple is unbreakable, and inside it (in survival) you can't place blocks or fluids, break anything, throw ender pearls, eat chorus fruit, or use Rick's portal gun. The Portal Device is the only way through.
  • Step on a chamber's glowing green exit pad to complete it. You can always get back out: drop into the water and walk to the door.
  • No monsters spawn inside.

Finding It

Being redesigned. The first version of the Trials wasn't Portal-like enough, so temples don't generate in the world yet. When the new Trials are ready, temples will appear in land nobody has explored, and the Explorer and Trials tabs have a "find it" quest.

Ops: /place structure cvages:puzzle_temple builds one where you stand.

The Chambers

  • 1. First Light: a ledge you can't climb. Two white panels: one by the door, one high on the wall above the ledge.
  • 2. Over the Gap: a pit too wide to jump, and a white panel on the far side.
  • 3. Fling: no white panels across the pit. Climb the ladder. A white patch on the floor below, a white panel on a wall facing the pit... speedy thing goes in, speedy thing comes out.
  • 4. Up and Over: three ledges. Each ledge's panel can only be seen from the ledge below it.
  • 5. Rocket: a ledge too high to reach, a white strip on the floor right beside it, and a ladder to a high platform over another white patch.
  • 6. The Long Way: chamber 3 again, but there's no ladder: get up to the jump platform by sight first.

Tips

  • Left click fires blue, right click fires orange. You can keep one portal and move the other as often as you like.
  • Speed carries through. Falling into a floor portal from high up shoots you out of the other one just as fast, sideways out of a wall or straight up out of the floor. Walking in barely gets you anywhere.
  • If you can see a white panel from where you stand, you can shoot it. If you can't, look for somewhere higher.
  • For the rocket: as you fly up past the ledge, hold forward to drift onto it.
  • Portals reset your fall: you don't take damage from the fall before the portal.

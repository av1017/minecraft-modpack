Talking to Villagers

Right-click a villager to open their menu. Their name floats above them, and the menu shows their mood and how many hearts they have for you.

MenuWhat's in it
TalkChat, tell a joke, tell a story, flirt, hug, kiss, apologize, hear rumors (they point you to nearby structures), play rock-paper-scissors
GiftGive the item in your hand. Good gifts earn hearts; giving the same thing over and over is worth less each time.
TradeNormal villager trading
CommandFollow me, stay here, move freely, ride a horse, set their home or workplace
WorkFamily and hired villagers can go prospecting, hunting, cooking, fishing, chopping, or harvesting for you
What works depends on their personality and mood: a joke cheers up one villager and annoys another. They also get tired of you if you talk too much in a row, so spread it out over a few days.
  • Make a villager a friend (40 hearts)
  • Ask for rumors and find the structure they mention

Dating & Marriage

  1. 10 hearts: give a Bouquet to start a relationship.
  2. 50 hearts: give an Engagement Ring to get engaged.
  3. 100 hearts: give a Wedding Ring to get married. Your spouse can follow you, work for you, and start a family with you.

Every villager has their own preferences, and some aren't interested; that's part of who they are. Rose gold versions of the rings work the same and look different. Divorce Papers end a marriage. A Matchmaker's Ring marries two villagers to each other.

  • Give a Bouquet
  • Get married

Babies & Kids

  1. Married to a villager? Choose Procreate in their Talk menu (they need enough hearts, and need a break of about an hour between babies). Engaged couples are asked to wait until they're married.
  2. You get a baby boy or girl as an item. Right-click to name it. You can't drop a baby, and you shouldn't try.
  3. Carry it for about a Minecraft day (20 minutes) until it says Ready to grow!, then use it on the ground and your child appears.
  4. Kids grow up over several hours of play. They love you from the start, follow commands, and can work once they're older. A Whistle calls your whole family to you.

There's a 5% chance of twins. Villager couples have their own babies too, and village kids sometimes ask you to Adopt them. Cribs come in every color and wood.

  • Have and name your first baby
  • Watch your child grow up

Marrying Each Other

The two of you can marry and have babies together, using chat commands. No cheats needed.

StepType in chat
Propose/mca propose <their name>
Say yes (or no)/mca accept <their name> or /mca reject <their name>
See waiting proposals/mca proposals
Have a babyOne of you types /mca procreate; the other types /mca procreate within 10 seconds
Split up/mca separate

The baby arrives as an item, just like above: name it, carry it until it's ready, then use it on the ground.

  • Get married in-game
  • Start a family together

Village Life

WhatHow it works
Running a villageRight-click a Blueprint while in a village to see its buildings and residents and set taxes. Villagers pay taxes in emeralds every so often.
Guards & archersSome villagers defend the village from monsters and raiders
InnsBring adventurers, wandering traders, and the occasional cultist
MailVillagers you know sometimes send you letters
InfectionA zombie bite can infect a villager; read Beware the Infection!
Death & revivalA villager you're close to leaves a headstone. A Staff of Life (Nether Star, End Rod, Scythe) brings them back.
Bad reputationHurt villagers enough and they hate you, and bounty hunters come looking
The Grim ReaperA boss fight. Death, and How to Cure It! explains it.
The mod has its own guidebooks. Craft a book with one extra item: a poppy for Relationships and You, wheat seeds for Managing Your Family, a bone for Death, and How to Cure It!, rotten flesh for Beware the Infection!, gold and copper for On Rose Gold.

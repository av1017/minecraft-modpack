Planes & Airships

Seven flying machines, from a pedal-powered copter to a flying warship. Build them from a few basic parts, fuel them with coal or wood, and bolt on upgrades and weapons. They work in the Overworld, the Nether, and the End.

In creative, they're in the Immersive Aircraft tab (click the arrows at the top right of the creative menu to reach later tab pages). To place one, right-click the item on open ground.

Biplanefast · needs a runway

Fast and fairly reliable. It needs a long, flat run to take off, and has a boost slot for rockets.

Airshipslow · easy

Slow but easy to handle. Rises and sinks straight up and down, so there's no runway needed. The best first aircraft.

Cargo Airshipslow · huge storage

An airship with chests built in: carries a whole storage room. Slow and burns a lot of fuel.

Warshipflying fortress

Slow, tough, and heavily armed, with lots of upgrade and weapon slots.

Gyrodynepedal power · no fuel

A muscle-powered copter. Pedal until the rotor is up to speed (the screen counts up the %), then lift off. Uses your hunger instead of fuel.

Quadrocopterfor building

A slow, steady little drone: hovers in place, which makes it perfect for building high up.

Bamboo Hopper3 seats · lands on water

A chunky plane with three seats and pontoons, so it can land safely on water. Has a boost slot.

Building

Fuel & Repairs

  1. Fuel goes in the fuel slot (sneak + right-click the aircraft). Anything that burns in a furnace works: coal, charcoal, wood, and so on. Blaze powder lasts the longest. The Gyrodyne needs no fuel; it uses your hunger.
  2. The screen warns you when fuel is low and when you run out. Land before then.
  3. Repair by right-clicking a damaged aircraft while standing next to it. Each click fixes a little and costs a bit of hunger.
  4. To pick one up, hit it until it breaks. It drops as an item along with whatever was in its inventory.

In Creative mode, aircraft don't burn fuel.

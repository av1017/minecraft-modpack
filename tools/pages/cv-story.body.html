Story & Adventures

Beyond the mastery quests, the quest book (L) has four more tabs. The Keeper's Journal is the story that runs through every age. Explorer sends you to every corner of the world, Trainer is all about your Pokémon, and The Keeper's Trials is a puzzle temple (its own page). Every quest pays out a Prize Crate.

The Keeper's Journal

Ada Thornwood was Cobble Valley's first Keeper: a trainer and explorer who mapped the whole valley, built a temple of puzzles, and then vanished. She left her journal in pieces for whoever came next.

  • Steps unlock in order, one chapter per age. The tab shows the next step once you finish the one before.
  • Each step gives you a page of her journal (a book you can keep and read) and a Prize Crate.
  • Some steps are group milestones (reaching an age); everyone who's done the steps before gets them when the group gets there.
  • The goals are real adventures: places to find, Pokémon to catch, trainers to beat, her Trials, the Nether, the End, and the Champions.

The journal pages themselves aren't printed here: find them in the game.

Explorer

Find every kind of place, from your first village to an End city, plus three side journeys: sailing (Small Ships), flying (Immersive Aircraft) and the deep dark. A place from a later age counts once your group reaches that age, so nobody gets late-game crates early.

Trainer

Catches, every type, a shiny, battles against wild Pokémon, trainers and each other, evolutions, levels, a full party and a trade. Counts are per player and add up over time.

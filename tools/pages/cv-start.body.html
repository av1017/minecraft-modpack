Cobble Valley is the Cobblemon pack plus our own mod, Cobble Valley Ages. It slows the early rush and makes the world feel learned instead of looted: the group moves through ages together, everyone gets better with the gear they actually use, and recipes come from quests, friends and instruction books. Two minutes here and you're ready.

The Short Version

Agesthe whole group, together

Every item belongs to an age: Wood, Stone, Iron, Diamond, Nether, End, Champion. The group starts in the Wood Age. First Steps (a crafting table and a wooden pickaxe) opens the Stone Age; after that, gym badges open the rest. Ages & Badges

Strange Objectsahead of your time

Things from an age you haven't reached show up as Strange Object and barely work. Carry them and use them and you'll slowly figure them out. Discovery

Skillyou, with each piece of gear

Tools, weapons and armor work at 35% when you've never used them and climb to 100% as you do. Your skill is yours, per item, and never goes down. Skill & Quality

Learning recipesquests, friends, books

Plain items are free once their age arrives. Gear, the furnace, the anvil and a few others must be learned: by a quest, or a friend who's mastered it teaching you. Furniture comes from instruction books. Learning Recipes

Disciplinescrafts you learn once

Cooking, Tailoring, Blacksmithing, Enchanting and Brewing. Each opens its stations (the cooking pot, smithing table, enchanting table...). Disciplines

Prize Cratesrewards with books inside

Quests, structure chests and gym wins give crates with instruction books and supplies for your age. Your first one arrives the moment you join. Books & Prize Crates

Nothing ever fails. Learners pay a few extra ingredients and make lower-quality gear, but a craft never eats your materials for nothing, and skill is never lost.

Your First 30 Minutes

Tick them off as you go; this page remembers.

  • Open your Prize Crate (Wood Age): right-click it. Read any instruction book inside the same way.
  • Punch a tree, make planks and sticks. The moment you hold 3 planks and 2 sticks, the Wooden Pickaxe quest completes and you can craft one (as a learner: a few extra planks, rough quality). Use it to get better. Same for the axe, shovel and sword.
  • Hold a crafting table and a wooden pickaxe: First Steps completes and everyone enters the Stone Age.
  • Before dark: a leaf bed (3 leaves over 3 sticks), a few leaf torches (leaves on a stick) and a campfire (sticks over logs). Bushcraft
  • Press L and open the Cobble Valley tab: that's the quest book. Each quest shows what to gather.
  • Pick your starter Pokémon and craft Poké Balls: catching, healing and the PC are all Wood Age. Pokémon Trail
  • Use your stone pickaxe and sword: reach 60% skill with them and the next tier's quests will master the new tools for you. Hover any gear to see your skill.
  • Learn a Stone Age discipline: Cooking (Farmer's Delight stations) or Tailoring (clothes). Disciplines
  • Find a gym and earn the group's first badge: that opens the Iron Age for everyone. Trainers & Gyms

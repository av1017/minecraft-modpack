Ages & Badges

The whole group shares one age. Reaching a new one makes its items recognizable and craftable for everybody, opens dimensions, and hands everyone a Prize Crate. Gym badges are the engine: every different leader anyone beats counts.

The Ages

"Items" counts everything in the pack that belongs to that age, worked out from every recipe: an item's age is the age of its cheapest way to make it. Gear without a recipe takes the age of its repair material.

Woodstart

Wood, leaves, sticks, wooden tools, leaf beds and torches, Poké Balls, the Healing Machine and PC. Most furniture books.

StoneFirst Steps quest

Stone tools, coal, copper, flint, furnaces (learned), Cooking and Tailoring.

Iron1 badge

Iron, gold, redstone and lapis; iron and steel gear; buckets, shields, Great and Ultra Balls (learned); Blacksmithing.

Diamond3 badges

Diamonds, emeralds, obsidian, ender pearls; diamond gear; the enchanting table; Enchanting.

Nether5 badges

The Nether opens. Netherite, blaze rods, nether wart, quartz, glowstone; Brewing.

End8 badges

The End opens. Elytra (no gliding before this), shulker shells, chorus fruit.

Championbeat every Champion

No new items: the bragging rights age, and a Champion Prize Crate for everyone.

How Badges Count

  • A badge is a different gym leader beaten by anyone in the group. Two people beating Brock is still one badge; Brock and Misty are two.
  • All the gym leaders in our pack count: the 8 Kanto leaders, the 8 Sinnoh leaders, and the 8 Radical Gyms leaders. That's 24 possible badges, and you need 8.
  • Sinnoh leaders have two fights (a first and a rematch); either one counts, once.
  • The Champion Age needs anyone to have beaten every Champion (the trainer mod's "defeat all Champions" advancement).
  • Badges won before you joined, or while you were offline, are counted when you log in.
Ops can check or fix the count: /cvages badges, /cvages badges add brock.

When an Age Arrives

  1. Everyone online sees the age's name as a title, with a fanfare.
  2. Its items stop being Strange Objects for everyone. Gear you'd already figured out keeps the skill you built.
  3. Its items become craftable (once learned, if they need it) and its quests open in the quest book.
  4. Each player gets that age's milestone in the Cobble Valley tab, and a Prize Crate of that age.
  5. The Nether Age and End Age open their dimensions. Before then, portals don't take you there ("You can't go there until the Nether Age").

Ahead of Your Time

You can find later-age things early: loot, trades, a diamond in a cave. Until your group reaches their age they're Strange Objects: the name is hidden, gear works at 5%, and a pickaxe you don't understand can't harvest ore.

Carry them and use them and you'll figure them out (Discovery). Once figured out, they work a little (10% rising to about 40%) and you can practice up to 25% skill, but you still can't craft them until the age arrives. Their tooltip says From the Iron Age, ahead of its time.

Quest Book

Press L (Advancements) and open the Cobble Valley tab. It's a tree: one node per age, and under each age the quests that become possible there. Quests complete by themselves when you have what they ask for. Everyone can do every quest, and each one pays out.

How Quests Work

  • Age milestones ("The Iron Age") are granted to everyone when the group reaches that age, each with a Prize Crate of the age.
  • First Steps is the group quest: anyone holding a crafting table and a wooden pickaxe moves the whole group into the Stone Age.
  • Item quests ask you to have things in your inventory (they aren't used up). If that's all they ask, the reward is the recipe: you can make it as a learner and get better by using it. Higher tiers also ask for 60% skill with the tier below, and those make you a master (full strength, full quality, can teach). Either way you get a Prize Crate.
  • Discipline quests ("The Art of Cooking") teach a discipline the same way.

Hidden Gear Quests

Every other piece of gear with a recipe (Epic Knights, Immersive Armors, Cobblemon Armors, the rest of vanilla) has a hidden quest. It doesn't show in the tab (and doesn't announce itself), but it works the same way:

  1. Your group has reached the item's age.
  2. You hold its ingredients (the amounts in its recipe).
  3. You have 60% skill with the matching vanilla piece of that age: the stone/iron/diamond/netherite pickaxe for tools, sword for weapons, chestplate for armor (leather in the Stone Age), the shield for shields, the bow or crossbow for ranged weapons.

With just the ingredients you learn to make it; with the 60% skill too you master it. Either way there's a 15% chance of a Prize Crate. Each piece's exact requirement is on Gear by Age.

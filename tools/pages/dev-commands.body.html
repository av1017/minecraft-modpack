Dev: Commands

Everything is under /cvages; /ages is the same command. Two parts are for everyone; the rest need op level 2. A key is an item id (minecraft:iron_pickaxe) or a discipline (cvages:discipline/cooking); tab-completion suggests them.

For Everyone

CommandDoes
/ages ageThe group's ages (ticked / not), the current age, and the badges so far.
/ages infoYour knowledge summary.
/ages info <key>Your skill, recipe status and discovery for one item.

For Ops

CommandDoes
/cvages info <player> <key>Someone else's knowledge of an item.
/cvages age unlock <age> / lock <age>Force an age on or off (unlock announces it like a real unlock).
/cvages master <players> <key> [quest]Instant mastery. quest marks it as quest-earned (what quest rewards run).
/cvages teach <players> <key> lesson|notesAs if taught by a lesson (20% skill) or notes (10%).
/cvages skill <players> <key> <0-100>Set skill exactly.
/cvages discover <players> <key>Figure out a Strange Object instantly.
/cvages forget <players> <key>Wipe someone's knowledge of an item (testing).
/cvages classify <key>An item's age, category, policy and discipline.
/cvages why <key>Why it has that age: the reason, and each recipe with its ingredients' ages.
/cvages dumpWrites config/cvages/age_dump.csv (every item: name, age, category, policy, discipline, recipe count, reason) and gear.json (gear with ingredients, for gen_quests).
/cvages badgesList the group's badges. badges add <leader> / remove <leader> fix the count (leader keys from badge_sources.json, e.g. brock).
/cvages lesson cancel <player>End a stuck lesson.
Creative and spectator players bypass everything (gates, penalties, locks), which is handy for building and testing but means you can't test the mod in creative.

Gamerules

GameruleDefaultDoes
cvagesEnabledtrueThe master switch. Off = vanilla behaviour (knowledge is kept).
cvagesBlockDimensionstrueLock the Nether and End until their ages.
cvagesPracticeRate100Percent multiplier on all practice gain (0-10000).

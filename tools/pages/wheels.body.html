Wear and repair

  • A bike has 100 durability. It loses 1 point every 100 blocks, and 10 for a fall of more than 4 blocks.
  • Below 25 it rides 15% slower. At 0 it's broken until repaired.
  • Lava destroys a bike instantly.
  • To repair, hold a Bike Wrench and right-click the bike. The wrench wears down by as much as it repairs.

Either of you can ride or pick up any bike. The bike's look (Style A or B) is in the PokeBike settings under Mods on the pause menu.

  • Craft a bike and ring the bell
  • Ride at night with the headlight on
  • Earn the Rainbow Bike (it takes a Nether Star)

How Cars Work

Every car is three things: a frame (the body), an engine, and a set of wheels. In creative, the Automobility: Prefabs tab has ready-made cars; just right-click one onto the ground.

  1. Craft an Auto Mechanic Table and open it. It has tabs for Frames, Engines, Wheels, and Attachments. Click a part to make it; it shows the ingredients you need.
  2. Place an Automobile Assembler and right-click it with a frame, then an engine, then wheels. Keep adding wheels until every wheel spot is filled.
  3. The finished car appears on the assembler. Right-click it to get in.
  4. Changing parts: a Crowbar takes parts and attachments back off.
Compare before you build: hover a car or part and hold Shift to see its stats: acceleration, comfortable speed, handling, and grip. Heavier frames are slower to get going; bigger wheels and better engines are faster.
  • Build a car on the Automobile Assembler

Attachments

Made at the Auto Mechanic Table. Right-click a car with one to put it on; the Crowbar takes it off. A car has one front and one rear slot.

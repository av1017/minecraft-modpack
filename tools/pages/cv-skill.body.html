Skill, Quality & Discovery

Three numbers decide how well a piece of gear works in your hands: your skill with that exact item, the quality it was made at, and whether you've figured it out at all. Everything here is per player and per item: your iron sword skill doesn't help your iron axe.

Skill

Skill only matters for gear: tools, weapons, armor, shields, bows, crossbows and tridents. Everything else just works.

GearWhat skill changes
ToolsDigging speed
Weapons (any mod, including Epic Knights)The weapon's damage bonus
ArmorArmor points and toughness (you can see it on the armor bar)
Bows, crossbows, tridentsArrow and trident speed, and so damage and range (40% speed at the very worst)

Hover gear to see both numbers: Skill 40%, working at 66%. Mastering an item (100%) also means you can craft it at full quality and teach it.

  • Progress slows as you get better, and there's a cap of about 2 skill points a minute per item, so grinding on one block doesn't work; playing does.
  • If someone taught you the item, you learn 50% faster; from written notes, 25% faster.
  • Most gear also has a quest: the ingredients alone teach you to make it; add 60% skill with the matching tier below and the quest makes you a master. See Gear by Age.

Quality

Gear you craft is stamped with a quality set by your skill with that item when you craft it, and the crafter's name.

  • Strength multiplies with your own skill: a Crude sword in a master's hand hits at 80%.
  • Loot, trades, creative items and anything from before the mod count as Masterwork.
  • Masters always craft Masterwork. Teaching someone or letting a master craft for the group is how you get the good stuff early.

Discovery

Items from an age your group hasn't reached are Strange Objects until you figure them out. Figuring out is personal, but it goes twice as fast once anyone in the group has done it.

You'll get "starting to understand", "starting to make sense", "almost understand" messages along the way. Once discovered, the item shows its real name, works at 10% and up, and you can practice it up to 25%. When the age arrives it becomes ordinary gear and keeps your skill.

Carrying alone can only take gear part of the way: to finish figuring out a sword you have to swing it.

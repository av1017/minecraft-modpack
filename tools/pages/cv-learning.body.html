Learning Recipes

Reaching an age makes its items possible; knowing how makes them yours. Plain things are free, gear and a few key blocks must be learned, furniture comes from books, and some crafts need a discipline. Learners pay extra ingredients and get lower quality, but never lose a craft.

Ways to Learn

Questslearn or master

The Cobble Valley tab (L) has a quest for most gear and key blocks. Gather what it asks and you can make the item as a learner; quests that also ask for 60% skill with the tier below make you a master outright. Every quest pays a Prize Crate, and everyone can do every quest. Quest Book

Lessonsfrom a friend who's mastered it

A master teaches you in person. You start at 20% skill, can craft it (with extras), and learn 50% faster. How

Instruction booksfurniture and decor

Right-click to read: every recipe in it becomes yours, fully. From Prize Crates. Books

Practicethe slow way

Using gear raises your skill, and 100% means you've mastered it. Crafting a must-learn item you've been taught raises it too.

Written notescoming in v0.2

A master will be able to write notes you read later (start at 10%, learn 25% faster). Not in the game yet.

Teaching a Friend

  1. The teacher holds the item they've mastered (for a discipline, its tool: see Disciplines).
  2. Stand within 3 blocks, crouch and right-click the learner.
  3. A bar shows the lesson: 10 seconds, plus 2 more per age (an Iron Age item takes 14 seconds).
  4. Stay within 6 blocks and keep holding the item. Walking apart, switching items, dying or leaving cancels it.
  5. Done: the learner can craft it (with extra ingredients while learning) at 20% skill, and learns it 50% faster from then on.

The Learner's Cost

While you're still learning, a craft takes extra ingredients on top of the recipe, and gear comes out at a lower quality. The extras shrink as your skill grows and vanish at mastery.

You areExtra ingredients
Taught by a lesson75% of the recipe's ingredient count, times what's left to learn
Learning from notes (v0.2)100% of the count, times what's left to learn
Learning a must-learn block50% of the count, times what's left to learn
A masterNone

Example: an iron pickaxe uses 5 items (3 iron, 2 sticks). Taught at 20% skill: 5 × 0.75 × 0.8 = 3, rounded up, so 3 extra items, taken from the most common ingredient first. Put more than one of each in the grid and the result appears; it shows its quality before you take it.

Learners need a crafting table (the 2×2 grid has no room for extras). Bucket-style ingredients that leave something behind are never charged extra.

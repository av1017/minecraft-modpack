Books & Prize Crates

Furniture and decoration are learned from instruction books, a few pieces per book, in every wood and color. Books come out of Prize Crates, which you earn from quests, find in structure chests, and win from gym leaders. Crates prefer books you don't have yet.

How Books Work

  • Right-click an instruction book to read it. Every recipe in it becomes yours, fully mastered: normal cost, any time.
  • A book is used up only if it taught you something. If you already know everything in it, you keep it: give it to a friend.
  • A book from a later age can't be read yet ("These instructions make no sense yet").
  • A book teaches a family in every variant. Some variants need a later age to craft (a crimson chair waits for the Nether Age) even though you know the recipe.
  • Hover a book to see what it teaches.
Furniture tools and workstations (Handcrafted's hammer, Another Furniture's hammer, Macaw's hammer and key, Beautify's botanist workbench) are ordinary recipes, no book needed.

Prize Crates

Each crate belongs to an age. Right-click to open: books of that age (ones you don't know yet, when there are any), supplies, and one Pokémon extra.

Ranges are random amounts; "2 of" means two picks from the list.

Where Crates Come From

  • Joining and new ages: each age milestone gives everyone a crate of that age, so your first Wood Age crate arrives when you join.
  • Quests: every visible mastery and discipline quest gives a crate of its age; First Steps gives a Stone Age crate; hidden gear quests have a 15% chance.

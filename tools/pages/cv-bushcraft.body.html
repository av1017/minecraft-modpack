Bushcraft & First Nights

Shears and wool take a while in Cobble Valley, and coal is a Stone Age thing. So the first nights run on what the forest gives you: a bed of leaves, torches of leaves on a stick, and a campfire with no coal at all.

Recipes

Leaf beds come in every leaf type (oak, spruce, birch, jungle, acacia, dark oak, mangrove, cherry, azalea, flowering azalea). The Leaf Beds mod's own recipe (3 leaves over 3 logs) works too.

Night One

  • Break leaves by hand until you have 6+ leaves: in Cobble Valley about 3 in 10 leaf blocks drop themselves (no shears needed). Grab saplings, sticks and apples on the way.
  • Make a leaf bed, right-click it to set your spawn, and sleep through the first night in it.
  • Make 8 leaf torches and light your camp.
  • Set down a campfire: cook raw meat on it without a furnace.
  • Got a farm going? Three hay bales over three sticks make a bed too.

To the Stone Age

Anyone holding a crafting table and a wooden pickaxe completes First Steps, and the whole group is in the Stone Age: stone tools, coal and charcoal, copper, and furnaces (learned by quest). Do it on day one.
